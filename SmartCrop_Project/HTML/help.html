📌 Frequently Asked Questions

What is SmartCrop?

SmartCrop is a smart farming platform that helps farmers with crop advisory, crop health monitoring, weather alerts and digital marketplace services.

How can I get crop advice?

Open the Crop Advisory page and enter soil type, temperature and soil moisture. SmartCrop will provide a suitable crop recommendation.

How can I buy or sell crops?

Open the Marketplace page to search available agricultural products. Sellers can add their products and buyers can view product details.

How can I check weather information?

Open the Weather page and select your location. You can view temperature, humidity, rainfall and wind information.

Can I receive weather alerts?

Yes. You can configure temperature and soil moisture thresholds from the Weather and Settings pages.
